Frequently asked questions about commercial insurance in Owensboro

What insurance does a small business in Owensboro need?

At minimum, most Owensboro businesses need general liability, commercial property, and workers compensation (required for businesses with one or more employees in Kentucky). Contractors also need bonds and commercial auto.

How much does commercial insurance cost in Owensboro?

Costs vary significantly by industry, revenue, and employee count. A small contractor might pay $2,000-5,000 per year for general liability, while a restaurant could pay $3,000-8,000. We get quotes from multiple carriers to find the best rate.

Do Owensboro contractors need a surety bond?

Many do. Kentucky requires surety bonds for certain licensed trades and public projects in Daviess County. We write bonds through multiple surety markets and can usually get approval within a few days.
