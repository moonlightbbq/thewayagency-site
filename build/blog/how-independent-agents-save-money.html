How Independent Insurance Agents Save You Money

By The Way Agency, Independent Insurance Agency · June 10, 2026 · 6 min read

When most people think about buying insurance, they picture one of two things: calling a big-name company they have seen on TV, or going online and filling out a form. In both cases, you are dealing with a single insurance company that can only offer you their products at their prices.

An independent insurance agent works differently. We are not employed by any one carrier. We represent dozens of them, and our job is to find the best combination of coverage and price for your specific situation.

Here is how that works in practice and why it usually saves you money.

Captive agents vs. independent agents

Understanding the difference between captive and independent agents is the key to understanding why the independent model exists.

A captive agent works for one insurance company. They can only sell that company's products. If that company's rates go up or their coverage does not fit your needs, the captive agent has no other options to offer you. They might be a great person, but they are limited by what their employer allows them to sell.

Well-known captive agencies include State Farm, Allstate, and Farmers. When you call them, you are talking to someone who can only quote their company.

An independent agent is appointed with multiple insurance carriers. At The Way Agency, we work with dozens of carriers across personal, commercial, and specialty lines. When you ask us for a quote, we can compare options from multiple companies and recommend the one that best fits your coverage needs and budget.

We work for you, not for the carrier.

How this saves you money

Rate comparison across carriers

Insurance carriers price risk differently. One carrier might be the cheapest option for a new driver in Louisville. Another might offer the best rates for a rural homeowner in Daviess County. A third might have the best commercial package for a contractor in Henderson.

A captive agent can only show you one price. An independent agent shows you multiple and explains the differences. This competition between carriers works in your favor.

Carrier-splitting when bundling is not the best deal

You have probably heard that bundling your home and auto saves money. Sometimes it does. But sometimes splitting your policies across two carriers, one with the best auto rate and another with the best home rate, saves more than any bundle discount.

A captive agent cannot do this. An independent agent can. We run the numbers both ways and recommend whichever approach actually costs you less.

Reshop at renewal

Insurance rates change every year. The carrier that was cheapest last year may not be cheapest this year. Independent agents can reshop your coverage at every renewal to make sure you are still getting a competitive rate.

With a captive agent, your only option when rates go up is to adjust your coverage or accept the increase. With an independent agent, we can move your policy to a different carrier that is offering better rates, without you having to start the process from scratch.

Discount stacking

Different carriers offer different discounts. Some reward bundling. Others reward claims-free history, home security systems, defensive driving courses, or professional affiliations. An independent agent knows which carriers offer which discounts and can match you to the carrier where you qualify for the most.

Better coverage, not just cheaper coverage

Saving money matters, but coverage matters more. The cheapest policy is not always the best policy, and an independent agent's value goes beyond price comparison.

Coverage gap identification

An independent agent reviews your entire insurance picture, not just one policy. We look at your auto, home, umbrella, and any specialty coverage together to identify gaps.

For example, if your auto liability limits are low and you do not have an umbrella policy, we will flag that. If your homeowners policy does not include water backup coverage and you have a basement, we will mention it. A captive agent focuses on their company's product. An independent agent focuses on your overall protection.

Claims advocacy

When you file a claim, your independent agent works for you, not the carrier. We help you navigate the claims process, push back if an adjuster's estimate seems low, and advocate for a fair settlement.

This does not mean we have adversarial relationships with carriers. We work with them daily and have strong relationships. But our loyalty is to you, and carriers know that independent agents hold them accountable.

Knowledge across products and carriers

Because independent agents work with many carriers and many types of coverage, we develop broad knowledge that specialists lack. A captive agent who only sells one company's products may not be aware of coverage options available from other carriers that would better fit your needs.

Common myths about independent agents

"Independent agents cost more because they charge fees." Most independent agents, including The Way Agency, are compensated by the insurance carrier through commissions, not by charging you fees. You pay the same premium whether you buy directly from a carrier or through an independent agent.

"Big companies have better claims service." Claims service varies by carrier, not by distribution channel. Some of the carriers we represent have excellent claims operations that rival or exceed the big-name companies. And when something goes wrong with a claim, you have an advocate in your corner.

"I can get a better deal online." Online comparison tools show you a limited set of carriers and often present bare-minimum coverage to show the lowest price. An independent agent compares more carriers, evaluates the actual coverage (not just the price), and provides advice tailored to your situation.

"I already have an agent, so I am set." If your agent is captive, you are only seeing one company's rates. It is worth getting a second opinion from an independent agent, especially if your rates have been increasing.

What to expect when you work with an independent agent

Working with us is straightforward:

  • Tell us what you need. We ask about your home, vehicles, family, business, and any specific concerns.
  • We shop the market. We compare rates and coverage options from our carrier partners.
  • We present options. We show you the best fits and explain the trade-offs so you can make an informed decision.
  • You choose. There is no pressure. You pick the option that works best for you.
  • We manage the policy. We handle renewals, endorsements, claims, and any questions that come up during the policy term.

    • When renewal comes around, we review your coverage again and reshop if it makes sense. You always have someone to call who knows your situation.
