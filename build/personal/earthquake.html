Personal Insurance

Do you need earthquake insurance in western Kentucky?

Get a Earthquake Insurance Quote

Standard homeowners insurance excludes earthquake damage. Western Kentucky sits on the New Madrid Seismic Zone, one of the most active fault systems east of the Rockies. Separate earthquake policies or endorsements are available.

Is earthquake insurance required in Kentucky?

Not required by law or lenders, but strongly recommended in western Kentucky due to New Madrid Seismic Zone proximity.

What earthquake insurance does NOT cover

  • fire following earthquake (covered by homeowners)
  • flood following earthquake (requires flood policy)
  • land and landscaping damage

What does earthquake insurance cost?

In our experience: $100–$800/year in western Kentucky. Key factors that affect your premium include: proximity to New Madrid fault zone, home construction type, foundation type, coverage amount, deductible (typically 5-15% of coverage).
