Personal insurance products

Each product page explains what the coverage is, who needs it, what it costs, and what it doesn't cover — in plain language.

Home Insurance

Covers your home, belongings, and liability. Required by most mortgage lenders. Does not include flood or earthquake.

Learn more

Auto Insurance

Liability, collision, and comprehensive coverage. Kentucky requires minimum 25/50/25 liability plus $10K PIP.

Learn more

Renters Insurance

Protects your belongings and liability as a tenant. Typically $15–$30/month. Many landlords require it.

Learn more

Umbrella Insurance

Extra liability protection above your home and auto limits. $1M coverage for around $200–$400/year.

Learn more

Flood Insurance

NOT covered by standard homeowners. Separate NFIP or private flood policies. Required in FEMA flood zones.

Learn more

Motorcycle Insurance

Liability, collision, and comprehensive for your bike. Kentucky requires same minimums as auto (25/50/25).

Learn more

Boat Insurance

Covers your watercraft, equipment, and liability. Not required by Kentucky law but typically required by marinas and lenders.

Learn more

Classic Car Insurance

Agreed value coverage for collector and antique vehicles. Requires limited mileage and enclosed storage.

Learn more

Earthquake Insurance

Western KY sits on the New Madrid Seismic Zone. Standard homeowners excludes earthquake damage.

Learn more

Pet Insurance

Reimburses vet costs for accidents and illness. Avoid choosing between your pet's health and a $3,000+ surprise bill.
