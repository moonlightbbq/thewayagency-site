What manufacturing typically need

Based on our experience working with manufacturing across Kentucky, Indiana, and Tennessee, here are the coverage types you should have in place:

  • Product liability
  • General liability
  • Commercial property
  • Equipment breakdown
  • Workers compensation
  • Business income and extra expense
  • Inland marine (goods in transit)

Kentucky-specific requirements

Kentucky manufacturers with distribution outside the state need product liability coverage that matches their distribution footprint. Workers comp rates vary significantly by manufacturing type.
