How does Medicare work in Kentucky?

Get a Medicare Quote

Federal health insurance for people 65+ or with qualifying disabilities. Original Medicare (Parts A & B) covers hospital and medical services. Gaps can be filled with Medicare Supplement (Medigap) or Medicare Advantage (Part C) plans.

Related coverage to consider

  • Individual Health — Individual health plans purchased through Kentucky's health insurance marketplace (kynect) or directly from carriers.
