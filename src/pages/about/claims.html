Claims advocacy

Filing an insurance claim can be stressful. When the unexpected happens, you should not have to navigate the process alone. That is where we come in.

We are on your side

As an independent agency, we work for you — not the insurance carrier. When you file a claim, our team steps in as your advocate. We review your policy to make sure you understand your coverage, help you document the loss properly, and communicate with the claims adjuster on your behalf.

What we do for you

  • Report the claim — We handle the initial filing with the carrier so you do not have to sit on hold.
  • Document the loss — We help you gather the photos, receipts, and records the adjuster needs.
  • Follow up relentlessly — If your claim stalls, we push to keep it moving. You should not have to chase your own insurance company.
  • Review the settlement — Before you accept an offer, we review it against your policy to make sure it is fair.
  • Explore your options — If a claim is denied or underpaid, we help you understand your next steps — including when it makes sense to escalate.

Real people, real help

You will never be transferred to a call center or left waiting for a callback. When you call us about a claim, you reach the same agents who wrote your policy and know your coverage. That personal relationship makes a real difference when you need help the most.
