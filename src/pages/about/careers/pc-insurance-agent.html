P&C Insurance Agent

← All open positions

We are looking for licensed Property & Casualty agents who want to be part of a team that actually supports them. Think more people like the ones already here: driven, client-focused professionals who care about doing right by people. If that sounds like you, we should talk.

KY, IN & TN Full-Time P&C Licensed (or willing) Client-Facing

The Role

You will serve personal and commercial insurance clients across Kentucky, Indiana, and Tennessee. That means quoting, advising, placing coverage, and being the person clients trust to protect what matters to them. You will work with a strong carrier network and modern agency tools that let you spend your time on clients, not paperwork.

Whether you are experienced with a book of business or earlier in your career and ready to build one, we have a place for you. What matters most is your character, your work ethic, and your commitment to doing right by the people you serve.

What We Are Looking For

  • Active Property & Casualty license (or willingness to obtain one)
  • Client-first mindset with strong communication skills
  • Ability to manage and grow client relationships
  • Comfort with technology and willingness to learn new tools
  • Team player who is willing to collaborate and support others
  • Self-motivated with a strong sense of accountability

Why Agents Choose Us

We built this agency to be the kind of place we would want to work. That means best-in-class compensation with no production ceilings and no red tape. It means strong mentorship from experienced team members who genuinely want to see you succeed. And it means modern tools and systems that handle the busy work so you can focus on what actually matters: your clients.

We are a team-first agency. No egos, no silos. Everyone here pulls in the same direction, and every person's contribution is valued. If you are looking for an agency where your hard work is recognized and you are treated like a teammate rather than a headcount, you will feel the difference here on day one.

What We Offer

Best-in-class compensation with no production ceilings
Strong mentorship and ongoing professional development
Modern agency management and quoting tools
Access to a broad carrier network with competitive markets
A collaborative, supportive team culture
Flexibility in how and where you work
A rapidly growing agency with real advancement opportunities
