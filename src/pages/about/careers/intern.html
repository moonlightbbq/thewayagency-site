Internship

← All open positions

We are looking for driven, curious college students who want more than a line on their resume. Our interns do not sit on the sidelines. You will take real ownership of projects that matter, with the guidance and mentorship to help you excel. When you leave, you will have something meaningful to show for your time and the confidence that comes with it.

KY, IN & TN Internship College Students Marketing, Business & Design College Credit Eligible

What You Will Work On

Depending on your skills and interests, internship projects may include:

  • Data Analytics — Help us analyze client data, market trends, and operational metrics to make better decisions
  • Marketing — Content creation, social media strategy, email campaigns, and SEO projects that reach real audiences
  • Graphic Design — Create marketing materials, social graphics, presentations, and brand assets
  • Sales — Learn how insurance sales actually work, from prospecting to quoting to closing, with hands-on experience alongside our agents

You will not be doing busywork. Every intern takes ownership of projects that contribute directly to the agency's growth, with experienced team members alongside you to help you succeed. You will see the impact of what you build.

Who This Is For

  • Currently pursuing a degree in marketing, business management, or a related field
  • Curious and self-motivated with a strong willingness to learn
  • Comfortable working independently while being coachable
  • Interested in how a modern business actually operates
  • Looking to build your resume with tangible, portfolio-worthy work

What You Will Get

This is the kind of internship that sets you apart. You will not be watching from the back of the room. You will have ownership over your work, a team that trusts you with real responsibility, and mentorship that helps you grow into it.

Strong mentorship — Direct access to experienced professionals who will invest in your growth
Real portfolio work — Projects you can point to in interviews and say "I built that"
Exposure to the business — See how marketing, sales, operations, and technology work together at a growing company
A team that cares — We are invested in your success, not just what you can do for us while you are here
College credit — We are happy to work with your college or university so you can earn academic credit for your internship (subject to your school's approval)
