Our Story

Founded in 1998, The Way Agency has spent over 25 years helping families and businesses across Kentucky, Indiana, and Tennessee find the right insurance coverage. As an independent agency, we represent top-rated carriers — which means we shop the market for you to find the best combination of coverage, service, and price.

Being independent means we work for you, not one insurance company. We represent top-rated carriers and match you with the right ones for your situation. Our job is to understand what you need, explain your options in plain language, and help you make a confident decision.

From the beginning, we've believed that great insurance starts with a real relationship. We don't operate a call center. We meet clients where they are — by phone, by video, or in person across the communities we serve. Our team has deep experience in home, auto, commercial liability, workers compensation, life insurance, Medicare, and employee benefits, with a special focus on contractors, trade businesses, professional services firms, and families throughout the tri-state area.

Coverage in Action

Insurance works best when you see it do its job. Here are a few real situations where having the right coverage and the right agent made all the difference. Details have been changed to protect our clients' privacy, but the outcomes are real.

A tornado, a roof, and a fight worth having

A Kentucky homeowner came to us after a spring tornado tore through their neighborhood and ripped shingles off half the roof. The initial adjuster assessment came back low, covering a patch job, not the full replacement clearly needed.

We reviewed the report, documented the full damage, and pushed back. We requested a second inspection and attended alongside the homeowner. The carrier approved the full replacement. Without an agent advocating on their behalf, they likely would have accepted the first lowball estimate.

The contractor who didn't know what he was missing

A small electrical contractor came to us for a routine quote. He thought his coverage was fine. We found significant gaps: liability limits too low for his contracts, no inland marine for tools, and workers comp classification errors that could have triggered an audit penalty.

We restructured his entire program. The total premium was actually lower, and his coverage was dramatically better. If a claim had come in under his old policy, the gaps could have left him personally liable for hundreds of thousands.

The family that saved by bundling smarter

A young family in Louisville had their home with one company and autos with another. By bundling with a single carrier, we cut their total premium by over 20 percent, and the new carrier offered broader coverage including better water backup protection.

Their previous policy had a replacement cost limit set five years ago and never updated. We corrected that, improved their liability limits, and still saved them money. That is what happens when an agent shops the full market and pays attention to the details.
