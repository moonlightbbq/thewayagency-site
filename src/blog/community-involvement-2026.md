Our agency is named after John 14:6 — "I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me." That verse is not just our name. It is the foundation for how we run our business and how we show up in the communities we serve.

Insurance is a relationship business. We live in the same neighborhoods as our clients. We shop at the same stores, eat at the same restaurants, and send our kids to the same schools. When our community is strong, we all benefit. That is why giving back is not something we do on the side — it is part of who we are.

Here is a look at how The Way Agency invested in our community throughout 2026.

## Supporting local organizations

Throughout 2026, we partnered with and supported several organizations in the [Owensboro](/insurance/owensboro-ky.html) area that are doing meaningful work:

**Local food banks and meal programs.** Food insecurity is a real issue in western Kentucky. We supported local food banks through financial contributions and volunteer time, helping ensure families in Daviess County have access to meals.

**Youth mentorship programs.** The future of our community depends on the next generation. We supported mentoring programs that connect young people with positive role models, life skills, and guidance during critical years.

**Community events and festivals.** Owensboro is a city that comes together. From Friday After 5 to the International Bar-B-Q Festival, community events are part of what makes this city special. We were proud to support and attend events that bring our neighbors together.

**Faith-based community outreach.** As a faith-based agency, we supported local churches and ministries that serve the community through outreach programs, counseling, and support services.

## Meeting clients where they are

Our agency model is built around accessibility. We do not have a storefront where you come to us — we come to you. Throughout 2026, that philosophy extended beyond insurance:

**Community education.** We believe that understanding your insurance is just as important as having it. Throughout the year, we shared practical information through our blog, social media, and community conversations to help families and business owners make informed decisions.

**Local business support.** We worked with small business owners across Kentucky, Indiana, and Tennessee to help them understand their coverage needs and find the right protection for their operations. Supporting local businesses is not just about writing policies — it is about helping them thrive.

**Accessibility and convenience.** We meet clients at their homes, their businesses, coffee shops, or wherever is most convenient. That is not a pandemic accommodation — it is our permanent approach. Insurance should fit your life, not the other way around.

## Why community matters to an insurance agency

Some people wonder why an insurance agency invests time and money in community involvement. The answer is simple: this is home.

Every policy we write is for someone we know or someone who knows someone we know. When we support a local organization, sponsor a community event, or volunteer our time, we are investing in the same community that trusts us with their protection.

Insurance is about being there when things go wrong. Community involvement is about being there all the time — not just when a claim happens.

## Our commitment for 2027

As we head into a new year, we are committed to deepening our community involvement. That means:

- Continuing to support local organizations that serve families and young people in western Kentucky
- Expanding our community education efforts to help more people understand their insurance options
- Looking for new ways to serve our neighbors beyond the insurance transaction
- Investing our time, not just our money, in the causes that matter to our community

We are grateful for every client, partner, and community member who made 2026 a meaningful year. Your trust in us is something we do not take for granted, and we look forward to continuing to serve this community in 2027 and beyond.
