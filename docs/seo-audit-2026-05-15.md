## Executive Summary

The Way Agency has the right strategic posture: independent, faith-grounded, SAB-light, narrowly geographic. The hardest decision (deleting 744 templated bridge pages) is already done. But six discoverability decisions are actively costing the agency its two stated priority markets, and four of the six are quick fixes.

**Owensboro.** A real `/insurance/owensboro-ky.html` page exists (sitemap line 42, lastmod 2026-05-13). It has no FAQPage schema, no Person agent schema, zero named licensed agent on the page, no neighborhood granularity (Whitesville, Philpot, Maceo, Masonville absent from data), and only five mentions of Daviess County. Live SERP for "best independent insurance agency Owensboro Kentucky 2026" returns Ohio Valley Insurance number one with the literal title `Best Independent Insurance Agency in Owensboro, KY`. The Way Agency is not in the top ten. TrustedChoice.com lists fourteen Owensboro indies; The Way Agency is on none of them.

**Mt Washington.** Hub page is in place (`/insurance/mt-washington-ky.html`). Webflow legacy URLs cleanly 301 (verified two-hop redirect chain via curl). Three local blog posts (`mt-washington-auto-insurance`, `-business-insurance`, `-home-insurance`) form a real content cluster. KFB Mt Washington wins local pack with three named agents and photos (Tom Sullivan, Debi Bass, Alex Short). The Way Agency shows zero named agents on the Mt Washington page; every team bio is "Bio coming soon."

**Single largest GEO issue.** Live `robots.txt` contains a Cloudflare-managed `Disallow: /` block for ClaudeBot, GPTBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, PerplexityBot via meta-externalagent, Bytespider, and Amazonbot. This block is not in the source `robots.txt`. Cloudflare is injecting it. Every AI engine that the audit targets is locked out at the crawler level. This is a Cloudflare dashboard toggle, not a code change.

**Expected impact window.** Local pack in Mt Washington shifts in 30 to 60 days (GBP-led). Owensboro organic top five is a 90 to 180 day fight against Ohio Valley and Allen Thomas. LLM citation rate moves the same week the Cloudflare block is reversed.

## Findings (abridged from the full audit)

The 25-item roadmap follows. Numbers map to the execution plan in `docs/seo-execution-plan-2026-05-15.md` (or the conversation transcript).

### Verified observations
- `/insurance/owensboro-ky.html` already exists. Has neighborhood prose (Smothers Park, KY-54, Pleasant Valley Road, Audubon Parkway, Owensboro Health). No FAQ. No named agent. No Person schema.
- `/insurance/mt-washington-ky.html` is canonical. `/mount-washington/*` 301s to it (verified via curl two-hop chain).
- All 5 team members in `data/team.json` have `"bio": "Bio coming soon."` and `"designations": []`.
- `data/seo.json:35` claims the team holds CISR designations not present in `team.json`. Fabricated trust signal.
- Live `robots.txt` contains a Cloudflare-managed block for 9 AI crawlers. Source `robots.txt` does not.
- Schema generators emit `streetAddress: "PO Box 187"` in JSON-LD on city and homepage pages. SAB violation.
- TrustedChoice.com/agent/ky/owensboro/ lists 14 indies. None of them are The Way Agency.
- BBB profile is filed under "Way Insurance LLC" at Mt Washington address. May indicate dual-presence or stale metadata.
- Mt Washington hub has no "or text" CTA on the hero (text is in the bottom contact-methods strip from `renderCTA`).
- Bourbon distillery blog post names zero Owensboro distilleries and never links to `/insurance/owensboro-ky.html`.
- Mt Washington business insurance blog post links to `/insurance/commercial-insurance-mt-washington-ky.html` which does not exist (broken internal link).
- Homepage hero does not link to `/insurance/owensboro-ky.html` or `/insurance/mt-washington-ky.html`.
- `_buildArticle` in `schema-generator.js` returns Organization author but is dead code in production. `generate-blog.js` line 310 already emits Person author.

### GEO citation share (live SERP, 5 priority queries)
- "best independent insurance agency Owensboro Kentucky 2026": not cited; Ohio Valley, Allen Thomas, Elite Risk Advisors, Best Insured, T.H.E. Insurance Group named.
- "best insurance agency Mt Washington Kentucky Bullitt County": cited third; KFB first.
- "independent insurance agent near Owensboro KY commercial": not cited.
- "flood insurance Owensboro Ohio River homeowner": not cited.
- "independent vs Kentucky Farm Bureau insurance Mt Washington": not cited; KFB pages only.

Citation share: 1 of 5 priority queries (20 percent). With Cloudflare AI crawlers blocked this likely drops further in pure LLM answers.
